## Follow-ups discovered (not started)
- [ ] `qemu_key`/`parse_combo`'s `"-"`-separated combo syntax can't express a combo containing the literal `-` character (e.g. "ctrl+-" for zoom-out) — `"ctrl--".split("-")` produces `["ctrl", "", ""]`, and empty parts are silently filtered, so the `-` is dropped instead of raising or being typed. Low priority (single-key `qemu_key("-")` and `qemu_type` both still work); would need a different combo delimiter or an escape convention to fix without breaking existing combo strings.
- [ ] `qemu_screenshot`/`qemu_wait_screen` both write to a fixed per-VM filename (`shot.ppm`/`wait_screen.ppm`) in the VM's workdir — fine under the MCP server's current one-call-at-a-time-per-VM usage, but two concurrent screenshot-family calls against the *same* VM (if a client ever pipelines them) could race on that file. Would need a per-call temp filename or a lock to make concurrent-safe; not worth it until there's an actual concurrent caller.
